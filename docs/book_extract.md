Source: *How to Make Over a Million at the Start of Every New Football Season*
by Justin Onyeka (Jay Soccer Predictions), pages 13–17.

This document is a summary of the rules — not a reproduction of the book's text.

## Book's other claims

- **Bet365 odds only.** The book states the analysis is calibrated for Bet365.
- **Season phases:**
  - Early season (Aug–early Nov): lots of goals, straight wins. Best phase.
  - Mid-season (Nov–Feb): erratic, avoid.
  - Late season (Mar–May): more draws, fatigue.
- **Avoid cup matches entirely.**
- **Stake strategy:** 4-odds accumulators, bankroll ÷ 4 = stake per ticket.
